# Kill criteria (2-3 weeks)

Kill or pivot if **any** of these hold:

1. Waitlist stays under ~30 real emails from the ICP with light outbound, **or**
2. Fewer than 5 people take a free pilot digest and give useful feedback, **or**
3. The only differentiation vs free/open-source monitors is check frequency or price

## Pass signals

- People forward the digest unprompted
- Specific “I would pay for X” language (not vague interest)
- Clear willingness to keep a short curated list rather than “monitor everything”
