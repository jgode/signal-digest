# Just the Diff

Public brand / domain: **justthediff.com**

# Positioning

## Promise

A human-readable weekly digest of *what actually mattered* on a curated watchlist  -  with named judgment criteria, clickable sources, and no filler.

## Not this

- Generic “watch any URL” SaaS racing Visualping / Distill / changedetection.io on price or check frequency
- Ping spam for every banner or copyright-year change
- AI slop articles dressed up as news

## Differentiator

**Authenticity and judgment.** Every item in the digest should answer: why does this matter to a real person this week? If we can’t say, it doesn’t ship.

## ICP (starting)

Founders, PMs, and competitive-intel freelancers who track AI/SaaS vendors’ pricing, terms, and product changes  -  and are drowning in noise.

## Monetization (later)

Follows usefulness: paid digest / seat after people ask for it. Not ads on day one.
