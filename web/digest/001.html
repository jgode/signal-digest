Just the Diff #1

Week of Sep 6, 2026

What actually changed on the pages that decide what you pay for AI and software tools. Short list. Links. Plain English why.

1. Anthropic made Claude Sonnet 5's cheap rate permanent

Anthropic had said Sonnet 5's $2 / $10 per million tokens (input / output) was intro pricing through Aug 31, then a jump to $3 / $15 on Sep 1. Their docs now say that increase will not happen. $2 / $10 is the standard price.

Why it matters: If you budgeted a September bump for Sonnet 5, you can stop. This also keeps the mid-tier "workhorse" model in a price war with OpenAI's mid models instead of stepping up.

Source: Anthropic API pricing docs

2. OpenAI cut GPT-5.6 Sol API prices (promo through at least Nov 21)

Sol is now $4 / $20 per million tokens (input / output) on the API: about 20% cheaper input and 33% cheaper output than the prior $5 / $30. OpenAI says the promo holds at least through Nov 21, 2026. Pro / Plus / Business subscription quotas are unchanged.

Why it matters: Flagship API spend drops for a few months, then may snap back. Treat this as a dated discount, not a forever rate.

Source: GPT-5.6 Sol model page

3. Cursor added a Teams Premium seat (and more included usage on Standard)

Cursor's June Teams pricing update (in effect for renewals from Jul 1) keeps Standard at $40 / user / month ($32 annual) with more included usage, and adds Premium at $120 / user / month ($96 annual) with 5x Standard's included usage. Seats can be mixed. Two usage pools: first-party models vs third-party API.

Why it matters: If a few people on your team burn most of the AI budget, you can buy them Premium instead of surprise overages for everyone. Admins also get clearer spend alerts.

Source: Cursor: Improvements to Teams Pricing

4. Linear made coding-session costs more explicit

As of Aug 20, Linear coding sessions bill model tokens at the provider's published rates (no markup) plus sandbox runtime at $0.25 per 20-minute block. Admins can set workspace and per-user spend caps.

Why it matters: "AI credits" stop being a black box. You can estimate a session from model rates + clock time, and kill runaway spend with caps.

Source: Linear changelog, Aug 20, 2026

Also noted (not ranked)

  • Anthropic Fable 5.1 cache reads are $0.25 / MTok in current docs (cheaper cache hits than Fable 5's $1 / MTok). Relevant if you run long agent loops that re-read the same context. Source: same Anthropic pricing docs.
  • Linear Sep 3 shipped Priority inbox and third-party app approvals on paid plans. Product change, not a price change.

How we picked

We only included items we could confirm on a vendor page or official changelog. Marketing fluff and unverified screenshots did not make the cut.
